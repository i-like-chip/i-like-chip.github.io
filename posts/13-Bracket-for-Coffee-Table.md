
Our coffee table gets moved around a lot, and it's a bit broken, so it kept coming apart. After glueing it back together a couple of times I decided to design some brackets.

![Table](./images/13-bracket1.JPG)

I designed the bracket to contour the wood at the corner joints.

![Bracket-1](./images/13-bracket2.JPG)

Each bracket is mounted with 3x 16mm socket-head-cap screws, with 8.8mm diameter head.

![Bracket-2](./images/13-bracket4.JPG)

I printed in this orientation with supports.

![Bracket-2](./images/13-bracket5.png)

The layers are compressed by the screws for best strength and durability.

![Bracket-2](./images/13-bracket3.JPG)

You can download the .stl here: [Bracket](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:7121202)
