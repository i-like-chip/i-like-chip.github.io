I found a stained glass style Korok on Thingiverse and wanted to make it for my partner's birthday. The original design had a loop for hanging it, but I wanted legs so it can be planted into soil.

![Korok](./images/14-korok-1.jpg)

For the stained glass effect I used epoxy resin mixed with acrylic paint. For the resin pour I needed a flat surface to stick the 3D print onto - I used a glass plate normally used as a print surface. On the glass I taped a square piece of plastic cut from a bin bag, as the resin will not bond to it. I then placed the print on top, and used a hot glue gun to seal around the edge to prevent leaks - epoxy won't bond to hot glue either. I painted the eyes and mouth black, and sealed them with resin.

![Korok](./images/14-korok-4.jpg)

I painted the legs black to blend in better with the soil.

![Korok](./images/14-korok-2.jpg)

Oh, I used glow in the dark fillament too!
